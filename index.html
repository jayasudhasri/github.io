Leadership approach
The right answer begins with the right question.
  • Frame before analysingDefine the decision, audience and trade-offs before committing analytical effort.
  • Make information usableDesign insight around how people interpret, discuss and act—not around the availability of data.
  • Challenge constructivelyBring evidence and judgement together to test assumptions without losing trust.
  • Prevent silent failureLook for weak signals, adoption barriers and consequences that sit outside formal reporting.
